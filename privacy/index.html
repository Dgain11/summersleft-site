Summers Left Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Summers Left does not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers.

What you enter stays on your device.

Your kids' names, birthdates, photos, interests, notes, and memories are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Ideas are generated on your device.

On supported iPhones, weekly ideas may be personalized using Apple's on-device intelligence. That processing happens on your iPhone and your information does not leave it.

Notifications are local.

Friday reminders are scheduled by your iPhone. No notification service receives your data.

Purchases are handled by Apple.

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

No tracking.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Deleting your data.

Delete a child in the app, or delete the app, and the data is gone.

Children.

The app is meant for parents. Information about children is entered by a parent and kept only on that parent's device.

Contact.

Questions: support@summersleft.app
